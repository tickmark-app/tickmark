# Code of Conduct

Tickmark follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, and keep discussions about the
work, not about people. Harassment and discrimination are not tolerated.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **tickmark-app@proton.me**. Reports are
handled confidentially by the maintainers, following the enforcement
guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
